## Vulnerator 2.0 — 7.5.2

**Merge Hardware opens your hardware inventory.**

### Merge Hardware
- **Fixed:** in 7.5.1, merging could stop with "Tick two or more hardware records that are the same device" even
  with two records picked.
- **Configuration Management > Hardware > Merge Hardware...** now opens a window with the whole hardware inventory.
  - **Find** looks up records by name, other name, registry number (`VUL-000123` or `123`), IP address or
    operating system.
  - Tick the records that are the same device. Clicking anywhere on a row ticks it. Records ticked or selected in the
    Hardware list start ticked.
  - With one record picked, Find starts with its short name (`W01` for `W01.Contoso.com`), so its other records show
    up straight away.
  - As you tick, the window shows the device they combine into: the one with the **oldest registry number**.
    **Show ticked only** lists just your picks.
- Merging works as in 7.5.1: the newest result of each finding is kept, the other names become the device's other
  names, and **Back up the database first** is ticked by default.

### Upgrading from 7.5.1
- Open your existing database as usual; nothing in it changes.
