# Looking Ahead

Planned for future releases of Vulnerator 2.0:

1. **More import formats** as DISA and the scanning tools change their outputs
2. **Report refinements** driven by eMASS template changes and user feedback
3. **Device tracking refinements** from real-world use of registry numbers, matching and Merge

Vulnerator 2.0 stays free and open source with no license keys. Integrations with outside services (Elastic, LLMs and
other APIs) are not planned; forks are welcome to add them.

Ideas and requests are welcome at https://github.com/hbonesctr/Vulnerator/issues.
